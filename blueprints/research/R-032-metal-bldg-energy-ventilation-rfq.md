## R-032.1 — Which energy code applies, and the climate zone

QUESTION: Which energy code governs a new Madison County Schools building on the HGHS campus, and what is Madison County's climate zone (IECC Figure / Table C301.1)?

ANSWER (one paragraph): **DCM is the code authority for K-12 school work, and DCM's State Building Code names ASHRAE 90.1-2013 (with three exceptions) as the energy standard, not the IECC** (S1). The statewide commercial rule lets a project use **either the 2015 IECC or 90.1-2013** (S3; DOE lists 90.1-2013, effective 2016-01-01, S4). Madison County's own permit side uses the 2015 IECC (R-006). DCM turned down the 2021 IECC / 90.1-2019 (S2). Adoption authority moved to the Licensing Board for General Contractors on 2024-10-01, with a proposed code due 2025-12-31, but **no newer adoption was found** (S1, S4, S13). **Climate Zone 3A** ("3A Madison", no asterisk, so not a warm-humid county) (S5). **Working rule for the RFQ: price an envelope that meets both 90.1-2013 and IECC 2015.** For metal-building roofs and walls in CZ 3 the IECC 2015 values are the stricter ones (R-032.2), so meeting the IECC values covers both. DCM confirms the path.
- **S1 (DCM list):** "ANSI/ASHRAE/IES Standard 90.1 – 2013 … with exceptions permitted to: 6.5.1 – Economizers, 8.4.2 – Automatic Receptable Control, and 8.4.3 – Electrical Energy Monitoring." Jurisdiction: "… on all public and private kindergarten through twelfth grade (K-12) schools …". DCM's list does not name the IECC.
- **S10 (IBC 2021 §1301.1.1, the base code DCM adopted):** "Buildings shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the International Energy Conservation Code." This conflicts with S1's 90.1-only list (see flags). IECC 2015 §C401.2 item 1 (S6) also lets an IECC project comply through "ANSI/ASHRAE/IESNA 90.1", so **a 90.1-2013 design satisfies both documents.** An IECC-only design satisfies S3 and S10 but may not satisfy DCM's list.
- **S3:** "Either the 2015 International Energy Conservation Code or the ANSI/ASHRAE/IES Standard 90.1-2013 shall be implemented and enforced for commercial buildings." Effective January 1, 2016.
- **S2:** DCM on the 2021 IECC / 90.1-2019 request: "… will not be adopting these codes during this code revision cycle."
- **S5 Table C301.1, Alabama column:** "3A Lee | 3A Limestone | 3A Lowndes* | 3A Macon* | **3A Madison** | 3A Marengo* …". Key: "A — Moist … Asterisk (*) indicates a warm-humid location." §C301.1: "Climate zones from Figure C301.1 or Table C301.1 shall be used in determining the applicable requirements from Chapter 4." Matches R-028 (S18 there).

## R-032.2 — Opaque envelope: metal-building roof and walls in CZ 3A (IECC 2015 C402.1.3 / C402.1.4) and the 90.1-2013 alternative

QUESTION: What do IECC 2015 C402.1.3 (R-value method) and C402.1.4 (U-factor method) require for a metal-building roof and metal-building / metal-framed walls in Climate Zone 3, "All other" (non-Group R) column, and is ASHRAE 90.1-2013 an allowed alternative?

ANSWER (one paragraph): **IECC 2015, CZ 3, All other:** metal-building roof **R-19 + R-11 LS** (liner system) or **U-0.035**. Metal-building wall **R-13 + R-6.5ci** or **U-0.079**. Metal-framed (cold-formed stud) wall **R-13 + R-7.5ci** or **U-0.064**. Using the R-value method on a metal-building roof requires a **thermal spacer block**; otherwise use the U-factor method. **90.1-2013 is an allowed alternative** (IECC C401.2 item 1; S3), and DCM lists it as its standard (S1). A manufacturer's copy of the 90.1-2013 CZ 3 nonresidential metal-building criteria reads roof **U-0.041 (R-10 + R-19 FC)** and wall **U-0.094 (R-0 + R-9.8ci)** (S11, SECONDARY). Those are less strict than the IECC values, so **an RFQ envelope at roof U ≤ 0.035 and wall U ≤ 0.079 meets both paths** (KEYSTONE comparison).
- **Table C402.1.3 (S6), CZ 3 "All other":** Roofs: insulation entirely above deck R-25ci; **Metal buildings (note b) R-19 + R-11 LS**; attic and other R-38. Walls above grade: mass R-7.6ci; **Metal building R-13 + R-6.5ci**; **Metal framed R-13 + R-7.5ci**; wood framed R-13 + R-3.8ci or R-20. Slab-on-grade: unheated **NR**; heated R-10 for 24" below. Opaque doors: **nonswinging R-4.75**. Notes: "ci = Continuous insulation, NR = No requirement, LS = Liner system." "Assembly descriptions can be found in ANSI/ASHRAE/IESNA Appendix A." (b) "Where using R-value compliance method, a thermal spacer block shall be provided, otherwise use the U-factor compliance method in Table C402.1.4."
- **Table C402.1.4 (S6), CZ 3 "All other":** Roofs: above deck U-0.039; **Metal buildings U-0.035**; attic U-0.027. Walls: mass U-0.123; **Metal building U-0.079**; **Metal framed U-0.064**; wood U-0.064. Slab unheated **F-0.73**; heated F-0.70. **Opaque swinging doors U-0.61.** Note: "Use of Opaque assembly U-factors, C-factors, and F-factors from ANSI/ASHRAE/IESNA 90.1 Appendix A shall be permitted, provided the construction, excluding the cladding system on walls, complies with the appropriate construction details …" and "Opaque assembly U-factors based on designs tested in accordance with ASTM C1363 shall be permitted."
- Column check: S11 (Thermal Design) prints the same 2015 IECC metal-building row (CZ 3 roof U-0.035 / R19+R11 LS; wall U-0.079 / R13+R6.5ci). Lamtec (S11) lists the same CZ 3 values (0.035 roof / 0.079 wall).
- **Liner system (S7):** "A continuous vapor barrier liner membrane that is installed below the purlins and that is uninterrupted by framing members. An uncompressed, unfaced insulation resting on top of the liner membrane and located between the purlins …" A plain screw-down roof with faced blanket draped over the purlins is **not** an LS assembly.
- **§C402.2.2 (S6):** "Skylight curbs shall be insulated to the level of roofs with insulation entirely above deck or R-5, whichever is less." "Insulation installed on a suspended ceiling with removable ceiling tiles shall not be considered part of the minimum thermal resistance of the roof insulation."
- **Cool roof §C402.3 (S6):** "Low-sloped roofs directly above cooled conditioned spaces in Climate Zones 1, 2 and 3 shall comply with one or more of the options in Table C402.3": "Three-year aged solar reflectance of 0.55 and 3-year aged thermal emittance of 0.75" or "Three-year-aged solar reflectance index of 64". "Low-Sloped Roof: A roof having a slope less than 2 units vertical in 12 units horizontal" (S7). **The 1:12 roof is low-sloped, so if the building is cooled this applies on the IECC path.** Lamtec (S11, SECONDARY) shows the 90.1-2013 metal-building cool-roof note for CZ 1 only, so the 90.1 path may not need it in CZ 3. UNVERIFIED in the standard itself.
- **Exemptions (S6):** C402.1.1 exempts buildings "that do not contain conditioned space" or with a space-conditioning peak "less than 3.4 Btu/h • ft² … or 1.0 watt per square foot". **A heated-and-cooled mat room does not qualify.** If the district only ventilates or lightly heats the building, the envelope rules change (90.1 "semiheated" criteria; UNVERIFIED, paywalled).

## R-032.3 — Does the 3,000 SF mat room trip the IECC 2015 C402.4.2 minimum-skylight rule?

QUESTION: Verify C402.4.2 exactly as written, apply it to the mat room using params values (show the arithmetic), and list the exceptions.

ANSWER (one paragraph): **Yes, as drawn the mat room trips the trigger.** It is 3,000 SF, which is more than 2,500 SF. It sits directly under the roof, and a wrestling practice room is a "gymnasium/exercise center" use. With an exposed roof (no ceiling drawn), the roof line runs from 16 ft at the eaves to 18.5 ft at the ridge, so far more than 75 % of the ceiling area is above 15 ft. **Exposed roof skylights are not the only answer.** Exception 5 (sidelighting) very likely clears it through the translucent wall band already in params. A continuous band on the 60 ft west wall with its head at 12 ft or more makes a sidelight zone of at least 60 x 12 = **720 SF**, which leaves 3,000 − 720 = **2,280 SF < 2,500 SF**. That only works if the band is real vertical fenestration that meets the zone rules and daylight-responsive controls are installed. The low-lighting-power exception (designed general lighting < 0.5 W/SF, i.e. **< 1,500 W** for 3,000 SF) is a second route. **Under 90.1-2013 (DCM's listed standard) the parallel §5.5.4.2.3 also trips** (≥ 2,500 ft², ceiling > 15 ft, "gymnasium, fitness/exercise area, playing area" listed). Its exceptions could not be read (paywalled), so whether the band clears it there is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **§C402.4.2 text (S6):** "In an enclosed space greater than 2,500 square feet (232 m²) in floor area, directly under a roof with not less than 75 percent of the ceiling area with a ceiling height greater than 15 feet (4572 mm), and used as an office, lobby, atrium, concourse, corridor, storage space, gymnasium/exercise center, convention center, automotive service area, space where manufacturing occurs, nonrefrigerated warehouse, retail store, distribution/sorting area, transportation depot or workshop, the total daylight zone under skylights shall be not less than half the floor area and shall provide one of the following: [1] A minimum skylight area to daylight zone under skylights of not less than 3 percent where all skylights have a VT of at least 0.40 … [2] A minimum skylight effective aperture of at least 1 percent, determined in accordance with Equation 4-4." §C402.4.2.1: "Daylight responsive controls complying with Section C405.2.3.1 shall be provided to control all electric lights within daylight zones under skylights."
- **Exceptions (S6), verbatim:** "Skylights above daylight zones of enclosed spaces are not required in: [1] Buildings in Climate Zones 6 through 8. [2] Spaces where the designed general lighting power densities are less than 0.5 W/ft² (5.4 W/m²). [3] Areas where it is documented that existing structures or natural objects block direct beam sunlight on at least half of the roof over the enclosed area for more than 1,500 daytime hours per year between 8 a.m. and 4 p.m. [4] Spaces where the daylight zone under rooftop monitors is greater than 50 percent of the enclosed space floor area. [5] Spaces where the total area minus the area of daylight zones adjacent to vertical fenestration is less than 2,500 square feet (232 m²), and where the lighting is controlled according to Section C405.2.3."
- **Arithmetic (KEYSTONE, params values):**
  1. Area: 50 x 60 = **3,000 SF > 2,500** ✔. Net inside the walls (8 in exterior, 6 in divider centered on x 50, ASSUMED): (50 − 0.667 − 0.25) x (60 − 1.333) = 49.08 x 58.67 ≈ **2,880 SF**, still > 2,500 ✔.
  2. Use: wrestling practice = exercise room (params use the IBC "exercise rooms 50 gross" factor) → "gymnasium/exercise center" ✔ (KEYSTONE reading).
  3. Ceiling > 15 ft on ≥ 75 % of the ceiling area: **IECC 2015 does not define "ceiling height"** (S7 checked). With no ceiling, the ceiling plane is the underside of the roof / liner, at 16 ft − d at the eave rising 1 in 12 to the ridge at y = 30 (d = depth of the liner / purlin zone below the eave line, TBD by Summertown). Height > 15 ft wherever y > 12 x (d − 1). The 75 % test holds while 12 x (d − 1) ≤ 0.25 x 30 = 7.5, i.e. **d ≤ 1.625 ft (19.5 in)**. So the trigger holds unless the liner / ceiling plane sits more than about 19.5 in below the eave line or a ceiling is hung at ≤ 15 ft. Measuring to the 14 ft haunch clear height would avoid it, but a frame haunch is a structural member, not a ceiling (KEYSTONE reading; DCM / architect call).
  4. If skylights are needed: toplit daylight zone ≥ 0.5 x 3,000 = **1,500 SF**; skylight area ≥ 3 % x 1,500 = **45 SF** at VT ≥ 0.40 (or effective aperture ≥ 1 %). Building maximum (C402.4.1: "The skylight area shall not be greater than 3 percent of the gross roof area"): gross roof ≈ 125 x 2 x √(30² + 2.5²) = 125 x 2 x 30.10 ≈ **7,526 SF** → **≈ 226 SF** max, or 5 % ≈ 376 SF with daylight-responsive controls (C402.4.1.2). Toplit zone around each skylight = "up to 0.7 times the ceiling height" each way (C405.2.3.3). IBC 2610 limits per R-030 (each ≤ 100 SF non-sprinklered, ≥ 4 ft apart).
  5. Exception 5 (sidelighting) arithmetic: needs sidelight zone > 3,000 − 2,500 = **500 SF** (> 380 SF on the 2,880 SF net). §C405.2.3.2 (S6): the zone extends "laterally to the nearest full-height wall, or up to 1.0 times the height from the floor to the top of the fenestration, and longitudinally from the edge of the fenestration to the nearest full-height wall, or up to 2 feet", with "The area of the fenestration is not less than 24 square feet", and no building or landform closer than its height above the band's sill. With head height H and band length L: zone ≈ (L + up to 4) x H > 500 SF. A full 60 ft west-wall band with H ≥ 12 ft gives ≥ **720 SF** ✔. A shorter band needs L + 4 > 500 / H (H = 16 → L > 27.3 ft; H = 12 → L > 37.7 ft). Band length / head height are **TBD** (the yaml only says "above 12 ft"). R-030 limits: plastic band only on walls with FSD ≥ 10 ft; continuous band needs FSD > 30 ft + CC1.
  6. Exception 2 (LPD) arithmetic: < 0.5 x 3,000 = **< 1,500 W** of designed general lighting. For scale, the code maximums are 0.72 W/SF "Gymnasium/fitness center — In an exercise area" = 2,160 W and 1.2 W/SF "In a playing area" = 3,600 W (Table C405.4.2(2)). Whether LED high-bays under 1,500 W light a wrestling room well enough is **TBD** (lighting designer; no illuminance target is set in params).
- **"Vertical fenestration" includes the translucent band (S7):** "Windows (fixed or moveable), opaque doors, glazed doors, glazed block and combination opaque/glazed doors composed of glass or other transparent or translucent glazing materials and installed at a slope of at least 60 degrees … from horizontal." ## R-032.4 — Air leakage / air barrier (IECC 2015 C402.5) for a PEMB

QUESTION: What does C402.5 require that a pre-engineered metal building must answer (air barrier, testing option, fenestration / door leakage, vestibules, dampers)?

ANSWER (one paragraph): **C402.5 is mandatory, and CZ 3A gets no air-barrier exception** (only 2B is exempt). The building needs **a continuous air barrier across the whole thermal envelope**, with every joint, transition and penetration sealed. It can comply in three ways: materials (≤ 0.004 cfm/ft²; "Sheet steel or aluminum" is deemed to comply **if joints are sealed**), assemblies (≤ 0.04 cfm/ft²), or **a whole-building test ≤ 0.40 cfm/ft² at 0.3 in. w.g. (75 Pa)** (ASTM E 779). Fenestration and doors need labeled leakage rates (Table C402.5.2), and OA intakes / exhaust openings need dampers. **Standard PEMB lapped panels with a vinyl-faced liner are not automatically an air barrier**, so the RFQ must ask how Summertown seals laps, eave / rake / ridge closures, base trim and penetrations. **Vestibules:** D1, D2 and D5 open into corridors under 3,000 SF (exempt). Doors straight out of the mat room (D3, D4) sit at the 3,000 SF line (gross 3,000 is not "less than 3,000"; net ≈ 2,880 is). Architect call. 90.1-2013 also requires a continuous air barrier ("Exceptions: 1. Semiheated spaces in Climate Zones 1 through 6 …", S12).
- **§C402.5 (S6):** "The thermal envelope of buildings shall comply with Sections C402.5.1 through C402.5.8, or the building thermal envelope shall be tested in accordance with ASTM E 779 at a pressure differential of 0.3 inch water gauge (75 Pa) … when the tested air leakage rate of the building thermal envelope is not greater than 0.40 cfm/ft² (2.0 L/s • m²). Where compliance is based on such testing, the building shall also comply with Sections C402.5.5, C402.5.6 and C402.5.7."
- **§C402.5.1 / .1.1 (S6):** "A continuous air barrier shall be provided throughout the building thermal envelope … Exception: Air barriers are not required in buildings located in Climate Zone 2B." "Air barrier joints and seams shall be sealed, including sealing transitions in places and changes in materials …" "Penetrations of the air barrier shall be caulked, gasketed or otherwise sealed …"
- **§C402.5.1.2.1 / .2.2 (S6):** materials "with an air permeability not greater than 0.004 cfm/ft² … ASTM E 2178"; deemed-to-comply list includes "Sheet steel or aluminum", "Fully adhered single-ply roof membrane", "Cast-in-place and precast concrete", "Extruded polystyrene insulation board … not less than ½ inch", "Foil-back polyisocyanurate insulation board … not less than ½ inch", "provided joints are sealed". Assemblies: "average air leakage not greater than 0.04 cfm/ft² … ASTM E 2357, ASTM E 1677 or ASTM E 283".
- **Table C402.5.2 (S6), max rate cfm/ft²:** windows **0.20**; swinging doors **0.20**; skylights (with condensation weepage) 0.30, all other 0.20; curtain walls / storefront **0.06**; commercial glazed swinging entrance doors 1.00; garage doors **0.40**; rolling doors 1.00; high-speed doors 1.30.
- **§C402.5.5 (S6):** "… other outdoor air intakes and exhaust openings integral to the building envelope shall be provided with dampers in accordance with Section C403.2.4.3." §C402.5.8: recessed luminaires in the envelope "IC-rated", "≤ 2.0 cfm … ASTM E 283".
- **§C402.5.7 Vestibules (S6):** required at building entrances. Exceptions include "Doors not intended to be used by the public, such as doors to mechanical or electrical equipment rooms, or intended solely for employee use", "Doors that open directly from a space less than 3,000 square feet (298 m²) in area", and "Doors used primarily to facilitate vehicular movement or material handling and adjacent personnel doors". Params: N-S corridor / entry 8 x 60 = 480 SF and E-W corridor 67 x 8 = 536 SF (D1, D2, D5 exempt); jan. / mech service door JD (mechanical-room exception); mat room D3 (exit) and D4 ("equipment pair", may fit the material-handling exception) need the architect's reading.
- **90.1-2013 (S12, SECONDARY quote):** "Continuous Air Barrier. The entire building envelope shall be designed and constructed with a continuous air barrier. Exceptions: 1. Semiheated spaces in Climate Zones 1 through 6. 2. Single wythe concrete masonry buildings in Climate Zone 2B." Test / leakage numbers in §5.4.3 not read (UNVERIFIED).
