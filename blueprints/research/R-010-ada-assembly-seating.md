## R-010.1 — Wheelchair space count for 2,200 fixed/assembly seats

QUESTION: How many wheelchair spaces does Table 221.2.1.1 require for a 2,200-seat assembly area with fixed seating?

ANSWER: **18 wheelchair spaces** (arithmetic from S1 Table 221.2.1.1).

Table 221.2.1.1 (S1) — Number of Wheelchair Spaces in Assembly Areas:

| Number of Seats | Minimum Number of Required Wheelchair Spaces |
|---|---|
| 4 to 25 | 1 |
| 26 to 50 | 2 |
| 51 to 150 | 4 |
| 151 to 300 | 5 |
| 301 to 500 | 6 |
| **501 to 5000** | **6, plus 1 for each 150, or fraction thereof, between 501 through 5000** |
| 5001 and over | 36, plus 1 for each 200, or fraction thereof, over 5000 |

**Arithmetic for 2,200 seats** (row “501 to 5000”):

1. Base for the first 500 seats: **6**
2. Seats in the incremental band 501 through 2,200: **2,200 − 500 = 1,700**
3. Incremental spaces: **1,700 ÷ 150 = 11.333…** → “or fraction thereof” → **12** (next whole number; see also 2010 Standards §104.2 calculation of percentages / fractions → next greater whole number)
4. **Total wheelchair spaces = 6 + 12 = 18**

Scope note (S1 §221.2): “Wheelchair spaces complying with 221.2 shall be provided in assembly areas with **fixed seating**.” Wheelchair spaces must comply with §802.1 (S2). This count is for the **spectator bowl total of 2,200** (locked). Luxury boxes / club boxes / suites are calculated **box by box** under §221.2.1.2 (S1) — **not applicable** while suites remain PARKED (D-030). Team or player seating: **at least one** wheelchair space in team/player seating areas serving areas of sport activity (§221.2.1.4) — **separate** from the 18 bowl spaces; product/layout TBD.

SOURCE: S1 (Table 221.2.1.1, §221.2, §221.2.1.1, §221.2.1.2, §221.2.1.4, §104.2); S3/S4; S5 (Alabama applies 2010 ADA).

EDITION / SECTION: 2010 ADA Standards §§104.2, 221.2, 221.2.1.1, Table 221.2.1.1, 221.2.1.2, 221.2.1.4; Alabama DCM adoption of 2010 ADA Standards (S5).

CONFIDENCE: **HIGH** for the table arithmetic (18) given a single 2,200-seat fixed-seating assembly count. MEDIUM on how AHJ / architect splits the 18 across MIX tiers (see R-010.4).

NEEDS ARCHITECT CONFIRMATION: **YES** (final seating plan, tier split of the 18, team/player space, and whether any box seating is later added).

## R-010.2 — Companion seats (§221.3)

QUESTION: How many companion seats are required for those wheelchair spaces?

ANSWER: **18 companion seats** — **at least one companion seat for each wheelchair space** required by §221.2.1 (S1 §221.3). No alternate ratio is stated in the cited Standards text for general assembly seating.

Companion technical rules (S2 §802.3):

- **Alignment (§802.3.1):** in row seating, companion seats provide **shoulder alignment** with adjacent wheelchair spaces; shoulder alignment point measured **36 in** from the front of the wheelchair space; companion floor surface at the **same elevation** as the wheelchair space.
- **Type (§802.3.2):** companion seats equivalent in size, quality, comfort, and amenities to seating in the immediate area; **companion seats shall be permitted to be movable**.

SOURCE: S1 (§221.3); S2 (§802.3, §802.3.1, §802.3.2).

EDITION / SECTION: 2010 ADA Standards §§221.3, 802.3–802.3.2.

CONFIDENCE: **HIGH** (one-to-one companion rule is explicit).

NEEDS ARCHITECT CONFIRMATION: **YES** (movable vs fixed companions at telescopic deck elevations — elevation match is a known bleacher design issue per agency forum discussion; Standards text itself requires same elevation).

## R-010.3 — Dispersion and lines of sight (§221.2.3, §802)

QUESTION: What must be true for dispersion and lines of sight? (No aisle geometry invented.)

ANSWER (requirements only — no KEYSTONE aisle layout):

**Integration (S1 §221.2.2):** wheelchair spaces shall be an **integral part of the seating plan** (within the seating-area footprint; not segregated outside the riser-defined seating).

**Lines of sight and dispersion (S1 §221.2.3):** wheelchair spaces shall provide lines of sight complying with **§802.2** and shall be **dispersed**. They shall provide spectators with **choices of seating locations and viewing angles** that are **substantially equivalent to, or better than**, those available to all other spectators. When the number required by §221.2.1 has been met, **further dispersion shall not be required**. Exception: team/player seating need not comply with §221.2.3.

**Horizontal dispersion (S1 §221.2.3.1):** wheelchair spaces shall be dispersed horizontally. Exceptions: (1) assembly areas with **300 or fewer** seats (not this project); (2) in row seating, **two** wheelchair spaces may be located side-by-side. Advisory: in an arena/stadium, horizontal dispersion means placement around the field of play / performance area.

**Vertical dispersion (S1 §221.2.3.2):** wheelchair spaces shall be dispersed vertically at **varying distances** from the screen, performance area, or playing field, and shall be located in **each balcony or mezzanine** that is on an accessible route. Exceptions: (1) 300 or fewer seats (N/A); (2) **In bleachers, wheelchair spaces shall not be required to be provided in rows other than rows at points of entry to bleacher seating.**

**DOJ assembly add-ons for stadiums / arenas / grandstands (S1 Note to Reader; S3):** in addition to §§221 and 802 —

1. Wheelchair spaces and companion seats are **dispersed to all levels** that include seating served by an accessible route;
2. Where horizontal dispersion is required and seating **encircles** (in whole or in part) a field of play or performance area, spaces and companions are dispersed **around** that field / performance area;
3. Spaces and companions are **not located on (or obstructed by) temporary platforms** or other movable structures, except when an **entire seating section** is placed on temporary platforms/movable structures in an area where fixed seating is not provided to increase seating for an event — then wheelchair spaces and companions **may** be placed in that section. When those spaces/seats are not needed for eligible persons, **individual removable seats** may be placed in them.

**Lines of sight technical (S2 §802.2):** where spectators are expected to remain **seated**, wheelchair spaces get comparable lines of sight over heads or between heads of seated spectators in front (§802.2.1). Where spectators are expected to **stand**, comparable lines of sight over standing spectators apply (§802.2.2). KEYSTONE does **not** invent C-values or aisle riser geometry here — see R-020 for tier C-value study; wheelchair eye-height check remains open there.

**Wheelchair space size / approach (S2 §802.1 — for overlay sizing, not a count change):** single space **36 in** wide min (adjacent pair: **33 in** each); depth **48 in** min front/rear entry, **60 in** min side entry; adjoin accessible routes; accessible routes and required circulation paths shall not overlap wheelchair spaces (§802.1.4, §802.1.5).

**Designated aisle seats (S1 §221.4) — note only:** at least **5 percent** of the total number of **aisle seats** provided shall comply with §802.4 and shall be the aisle seats closest to accessible routes. Count = TBD until the seating plan fixes how many aisle seats exist; **not** part of the 18+18 figure above.

SOURCE: S1 (§221.2.2, §221.2.3, §221.2.3.1, §221.2.3.2, §221.4, DOJ Note to Reader); S2 (§802.1, §802.2, §802.4); S3.

EDITION / SECTION: 2010 ADA Standards §§221.2.2–221.2.3.2, 221.4, 802.1–802.2, 802.4; 28 CFR 35.151 / 36.406 assembly-area provisions as printed in S3.

CONFIDENCE: **HIGH** that the cited obligations apply to a new arena with fixed seating. **LOW** on any specific aisle/vomitory geometry (not researched; do not invent).

NEEDS ARCHITECT CONFIRMATION: **YES** (dispersion map on P2-A-103 / seating overlays; standing vs seated event assumption for §802.2; bleacher entry-point locations for the telescopic tier).

## R-010.4 — Does telescopic / MIX seating (D-009) change the Table 221.2.1.1 count?

QUESTION: D-009 is MIX (telescopic lower tier + fixed upper tier, 1,100 + 1,100 ASSUMED split, total 2,200). Does that change the required wheelchair-space **number**?

ANSWER: **The cited Standards do not publish a different Table 221.2.1.1 formula for telescopic or MIX seating.** §221.2 scopes wheelchair spaces to assembly areas with **fixed seating**; telescopic / folding bleachers, when provided as spectator seating, are treated under the assembly-seating provisions (bleacher-specific vertical-dispersion exception at §221.2.3.2 Exception 2; DOJ temporary-platform rule for movable structures — S1/S3). Agency materials discuss bleacher/telescopic **design** issues (companion elevation, integration, sightlines — S6 webinar topic list) but do **not** replace Table 221.2.1.1.

**KEYSTONE application (ASSUMED — mark on sheets):**

- Apply Table 221.2.1.1 **once** to the locked bowl total **2,200** → **18** wheelchair spaces + **18** companions (R-010.1 / R-010.2).
- Do **not** invent a separate 1,100 + 1,100 double-count unless the architect / AHJ directs that each tier is a separate assembly area (Standards text for general seating is by number of seats in the assembly area; luxury-box separate counting is explicit only for boxes/suites under §221.2.1.2 — N/A while suites PARKED).
- MIX **does** affect **where** the 18 go (horizontal around the floor; vertical / entry-point rules for the telescopic bleacher tier; companion same-elevation on telescopic decks) — that is layout, not a new count.
- ICC 300 (bleachers / folding and telescopic seating) may add product/installation rules — **not read** (paywalled; same gap as R-007/R-008). Mark **TBD**.

SOURCE: S1 (§221.2, Table 221.2.1.1, §221.2.3.2 Exception 2, DOJ Note to Reader item 3); S3; S6 (context only). D-009 = locked project decision (MIX), not a code source.

EDITION / SECTION: 2010 ADA Standards §§221.2, 221.2.1.1, 221.2.3.2 Exc. 2; DOJ assembly temporary-platform rule (S3).

CONFIDENCE: **HIGH** that Table 221.2.1.1 still yields **18** for 2,200 seats. **MEDIUM** that a single bowl application (not per-tier double count) is the correct read — **ASSUMED** until architect/AHJ confirms. **TBD** on ICC 300 interaction.
